# Revenue Workflow Audits — Batch 001

## Audit 001 — Eagles HVAC Services

**Company:** Eagles HVAC Services  
**Location:** Aldie, Virginia  
**Decision maker:** Gurguis (Guirguis) Faltaos — Owner  
**Decision-maker verification:** BBB lists Mr. Gurguis Faltaos as owner and principal contact.  
**Primary website:** https://www.eagleshvac.com/

### Observed customer-entry points

- Public phone number: 571-354-1559.
- Website contact form collects name, phone, email, service need, new/existing customer status, referral source, SMS capability, and message.
- The contact form explicitly requests consent for SMS/email informational, appointment, and marketing messages.
- The site promotes scheduling/service contact, free in-home equipment estimates, financing, maintenance plans, and emergency service.
- Multiple service/location pages repeat the same lead form.

### Verified workflow observations

1. The website has a relatively rich inbound capture form rather than a phone-only path.
2. The same form is reused across multiple service pages, creating a centralized-looking intake point from the public-facing site.
3. The site explicitly states that submitted messages are received and that the company will reach out as soon as possible.
4. The public site does not let us verify the internal routing, response-time SLA, CRM ownership, appointment-confirmation workflow, or estimate follow-up sequence.

### Revenue-workflow hypothesis

The opportunity is not that the website is visibly broken. The opportunity is to determine whether inbound inquiries, estimate requests, and other customer requests are consistently routed, acknowledged, followed up, and measured after submission.

Because the site already captures SMS/email permission and promotes estimates, maintenance, and multiple service categories, there is a reasonable diagnostic question around what happens between **form submission → human response → appointment/estimate → follow-up**.

### Evidence still required

- Actual post-submission confirmation behavior.
- Internal notification/routing destination.
- First-response time.
- Whether unbooked inquiries receive automated or manual follow-up.
- Whether estimates/proposals are tracked and followed up.
- CRM or field-service software used for lead ownership and status.
- Stale-lead handling.

### Audit status

**READY FOR PERSONALIZED OUTREACH + DISCOVERY CALL**  
The message should frame the finding as a workflow question, not claim lost revenue or poor performance.

## Audit 002 — USA General Contractors, Inc.

**Company:** USA General Contractors, Inc.  
**Location:** Gainesville, Virginia  
**Decision maker:** Mynor R. Morales — Owner  
**Decision-maker verification:** BBB lists Mr. Mynor R. Morales as owner and principal/customer contact.  
**Primary website:** https://www.usageneralcontractors.com/

### Observed customer-entry points

- Public phone number: (866) 303-6383.
- General contact page has a form for first name, last name, email, phone number, and message.
- The contact page explicitly directs customers who want to schedule service to a separate **Schedule Online** page.
- The company website markets HVAC alongside other home/commercial services.

### Verified workflow observations

1. The public site clearly distinguishes between a general-contact path and an online scheduling path.
2. The contact page tells visitors to use the separate scheduling path for service scheduling.
3. We can verify the existence of both entry points, but we cannot verify whether both feed the same CRM, notification queue, lead owner, or follow-up sequence.
4. We cannot publicly verify first-response time, booking abandonment handling, estimate follow-up, or stale-lead recovery.

### Revenue-workflow hypothesis

The key diagnostic question is whether **general inquiries and scheduled-service requests are unified into one tracked customer workflow**.

If they are handled through separate systems or queues, there may be a handoff/visibility issue worth investigating. If they are already unified, the audit should move downstream to response time, booking completion, estimate follow-up, and stale-lead handling.

### Evidence still required

- Exact scheduling platform and CRM/field-service system.
- Whether general-contact submissions create CRM records automatically.
- Whether schedule-online submissions create the same type of record.
- Internal notification and ownership rules.
- First-response time.
- Booking confirmation/reminder sequence.
- Estimate/proposal follow-up process.
- Stale-lead recovery process.

### Audit status

**READY FOR PERSONALIZED OUTREACH + DISCOVERY CALL**  
The message should ask whether the two entry paths are already unified rather than implying that they are not.

## Evidence standard

These audits intentionally separate **Observed**, **Not publicly verifiable**, and **Hypothesis**. No specific lost-revenue figure, conversion-rate claim, response-time claim, case study, or performance guarantee should be used until supported by prospect data.
