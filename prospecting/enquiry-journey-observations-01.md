# Revenue Recovery Lab — Public Enquiry Journey Observations 01

Research date: 2026-11-08
Scope: Publicly visible website pages only. No forms submitted, no calls made, and no response-time tests performed.
Important: These observations describe visible design, not proof of missed leads or slow response.

| Company | Public journey observed | Visible friction / evidence | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adonox Marketing | Contact page offers email, WhatsApp and a form. Form requests name, email, phone, company and message. It also displays a simple anti-spam arithmetic field. | Multiple channels give prospects choice. The form has five business fields plus anti-spam. Page says the team will respond promptly but gives no measurable response-time commitment. This does NOT prove slow response. | No defensible lead-loss finding. A possible audit question is how enquiries from the three channels are assigned and followed up, but this must be asked, not assumed. |
| B2B LAB | Contact page offers a free B2B marketing conversation and a form requesting name, surname, email, phone, website link and message. | Six fields before submission; public email is also shown. No response-time commitment visible in the page text. | Slightly heavier form, but no evidence it reduces conversion or that follow-up is weak. |
| b2Target | Contact page offers a form, email, phone, and a free 30-minute introductory conversation CTA. Form asks name, email, phone and message. | Multiple contact routes and a clear meeting offer. No response-time commitment visible in the page text. | Strong visible contact options; not an obvious audit pain point from public evidence alone. |
| BarkB2B | About page has a contact CTA and public email. It positions itself as a boutique B2B agency and invites partnership enquiries. | The opened page did not expose the full enquiry form or any response-time expectation. | Not enough evidence. Inspect contact page separately before any approach. |
| Emogy | Contact page provides email and phone plus a form requesting name, email, subject, message and privacy consent. Business hours are displayed. | Four required content fields plus consent. No online booking or response-time promise visible in the captured page text. | No evidence of a broken journey. Possible question: how are enquiries received outside displayed office hours handled? Do not assume they are missed. |

## What this means for prospecting
- None of these pages proves a lead-response failure.
- Publicly visible form length, multiple channels, or lack of a response-time promise is not sufficient evidence of lost revenue.
- Do not send a pitch claiming that any of these agencies is losing leads.
- The next responsible move is to test the offer through a neutral, permission-based conversation: ask whether they would find an independent review useful, without alleging a problem.
- Consider a better-fit segment if the target agencies already sell funnel optimization, CRM implementation, demand generation, or sales operations as core services.
