## Qualification rules

Prioritize prospects with (1) a small or mid-sized agency structure, (2) B2B enquiry generation or multiple service lines, (3) a publicly verifiable senior contact, and (4) a credible complementary use for an enquiry-journey audit and follow-up workflow. Public website design is not evidence of slow response or lost revenue. Never claim that a prospect is losing leads without direct evidence.

Offer to discuss a bounded, evidence-led review of one enquiry journey. Do not request credentials, customer lists, or unredacted personal data. Do not promise revenue recovery.

## Outreach safeguards and next step

1. These are drafts only. Nothing has been sent.
2. Before contacting EU-based prospects (Poland, Netherlands, Germany), check applicable direct-marketing/privacy rules and use an appropriate, permission-based professional channel; do not bulk-send unsolicited commercial email.
3. Do not invent personal email formats or scrape private contact data. Use public business contact routes or a public professional profile, and ask to be directed to the right person when needed.
4. Do not attach a report alleging missed leads. Offer a short scope note first.
5. Log each attempt, date, channel, response and opt-out. One respectful follow-up at most, then stop.
6. Before any outreach, confirm the sender channel is genuinely accessible to the user. No verified business email has yet been established.
7. First commercial ask: a short scoping conversation, not an immediate $149 checkout. Keep the $149 pilot price for when the prospect confirms a real need and scope.
