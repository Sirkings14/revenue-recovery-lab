## Sending and compliance controls
- Send one message per organization, to the published business contact route only.
- Do not send to multiple addresses at the same organization.
- Do not infer a response-time issue, lost revenue, or poor lead handling from the public website.
- Use a truthful sender identity and accurate subject; include a clear opt-out line in the final message if applicable to the recipient jurisdiction and sending channel.
- Respect local direct-marketing/privacy rules; a published address is not blanket consent.
- One respectful follow-up at most after 4–5 business days; suppress immediately on objection or opt-out.
- No attachments, tracking pixels, bulk campaigns, or unsupported revenue claims.
- Website deployment and customer case studies are not yet verified; do not claim either.
