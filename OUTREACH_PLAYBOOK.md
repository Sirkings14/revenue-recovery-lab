# Stage 4D — Outreach System

## Objective

Turn a verified revenue-workflow observation into a short, credible conversation with the person responsible for revenue, sales, operations, or customer acquisition.

The goal of the first message is **not** to sell a $5,000 implementation.

The goal is to earn permission for a short diagnostic conversation.

## Core positioning

Revenue Recovery Lab helps HVAC companies identify and automate gaps between inquiry, qualification, follow-up, booking, pipeline management, and conversion.

The outreach must be based on a real observation.

## Required personalization fields

Before sending, the operator should know:

- company name
- decision-maker name
- decision-maker role
- website
- one concrete workflow observation
- page or source where the observation was found
- why the observation is relevant to revenue operations

If these are missing, do not send personalized outreach yet.

## Outreach structure

1. Relevant observation
2. Why it caught our attention
3. Low-pressure question
4. No exaggerated claim
5. No invented financial loss

## First-touch template

Subject: Quick question about {{company}}'s lead follow-up

Hi {{first_name}},

I was looking at {{company}}'s customer journey and noticed {{specific_observation}}.

I work on revenue workflows for service businesses—mainly the gaps between a new inquiry, follow-up, booking and the sales pipeline.

I was curious whether {{observation_question}} is something your team already has handled internally.

If not, I can send you the two-minute version of what I noticed and what I would test first.

— Revenue Recovery Lab

## Follow-up 1

Send only if there is no response and the contact method permits follow-up.

Hi {{first_name}},

Following up on this because the issue I noticed was specifically around {{workflow_area}}.

Not trying to pitch a generic marketing service—I was checking whether this part of the customer journey is already being measured and followed up consistently.

Worth sending over the observation?

## Follow-up 2

Hi {{first_name}},

Last note from me.

If {{workflow_area}} is already running well, no problem. If it isn't, I can document the gap and show you what I would change before suggesting any implementation.

Either way, happy to leave it here.

## Response classification

Every reply should be classified immediately:

- `interested` — wants to discuss
- `question` — asks for clarification
- `send_info` — requests the observation/audit
- `not_now` — timing objection
- `not_relevant` — problem does not apply
- `referral` — points to another person
- `no_fit` — clearly outside target
- `unsubscribe` — stop outreach immediately

## Interested response

Move the prospect to a diagnostic conversation.

Do not jump directly to price.

Diagnostic objectives:

1. Understand current lead sources.
2. Understand what happens after an inquiry.
3. Understand how estimates/proposals are followed up.
4. Identify ownership of the process.
5. Identify existing CRM/software.
6. Establish current baseline metrics when the prospect can provide them.
7. Confirm whether the problem is worth fixing.

## Evidence standard

Never tell a prospect that they are losing a specific amount of money unless the prospect's own data supports the calculation.

Use language such as:

- "I observed..."
- "I could not verify..."
- "This may create a follow-up gap..."
- "I'd want to measure..."

Avoid:

- "You're losing $50,000/month."
- "We guarantee 30% more sales."
- "Your competitors are stealing your customers."
- fabricated case studies or testimonials.

## Sales transition

The commercial sequence is:

Observation → Diagnostic → Baseline → Scope → Proposal → Implementation → Measurement

The implementation proposal should describe:

- workflow being changed
- systems involved
- automations to be configured
- human approval points
- tracking/measurement
- implementation timeline
- fee
- optional ongoing optimization

## Compliance and respect

Use legitimate business contact information and comply with applicable email, messaging, privacy, and marketing rules. Honor opt-outs and do not continue contacting people who ask to stop.

## Stage 4D success condition

The system is ready for controlled outreach when we have:

- 10 verified decision-makers
- 10 evidence-backed observations
- 10 personalized first-touch messages
- response classifications ready
- diagnostic questions ready
