# Personalized Prospect Outreach — Revenue Recovery Lab

Use these as starting points only. Personalize every message using a verifiable observation. Do not send if you cannot identify a real, relevant issue. Respect opt-outs and applicable marketing/privacy rules.

## Email 1 — Permission-based first contact

**Subject:** Quick question about [Company]'s enquiry follow-up

Hi [First name],

I was looking at [specific page or enquiry route] on [Company]'s website and noticed [one precise, verifiable observation].

I work with small agencies on the process between a new enquiry and the next follow-up. I may be wrong about how your internal process works, but this public-facing step made me wonder whether there is an opportunity to make the handoff clearer.

Would it be useful if I sent over a short, no-pressure review of that one journey?

## Rules before sending

- Verify the company, recipient role and observation.
- Use a public business contact route and comply with applicable law and platform rules.
- Never imply you tested a private CRM, inbox or internal workflow when you did not.
- Do not attach a report containing invented findings.
- Do not claim the business is losing a specific amount of money without evidence.
- One follow-up maximum unless the recipient engages.
- Record the date, channel, message, response and opt-out status.
