# Payment Route Decision

## Current position

Revenue Recovery Lab should not route client money through another person's Payoneer account merely because that person is related to the operator.

The account holder, contracting party, invoice issuer, service provider, and payment recipient should have a legitimate and documented relationship.

## Payoneer route

Payoneer currently supports payment requests/invoices to global clients, subject to account eligibility and regional availability. Clients can use supported methods such as card, ACH debit, bank transfer, or PayPal depending on the request and eligibility.

Before using a Payoneer account for Revenue Recovery Lab, verify inside the account:

1. Payment Request / Get Paid is enabled.
2. Business or professional activity is correctly represented.
3. The website accurately represents the business activity.
4. The intended client/service transactions are permitted.
5. Receiving and withdrawal options are available for the account holder.
6. Current fees and transaction limits are understood.

## Brother's Nigerian account

A brother's verified Nigerian Payoneer account can only be part of the payment architecture if the underlying business relationship is genuine and documented.

Possible legitimate structure:
- brother is a genuine business partner or contracting entity;
- contracts/invoices accurately identify the service provider;
- revenue-sharing or subcontracting terms are documented;
- both parties understand tax, accounting, refund, dispute, and compliance responsibilities;
- Payoneer confirms the intended activity is permitted.

Do not represent the brother as the operator of Revenue Recovery Lab if he is not actually performing that role.

Do not use his account as a pass-through merely to bypass geographic availability restrictions.

## Client payment sequence

Preferred sequence:

```text
Qualified client
    -> proposal accepted
    -> contract signed
    -> invoice/payment request issued by the legitimate contracting party
    -> client pays
    -> payment confirmed
    -> onboarding starts
    -> implementation begins
```

## Backup route

Maintain a second compliant payment option if the primary provider does not support the operator's jurisdiction or intended business structure.

Any backup provider must be checked for:
- country availability;
- business/service eligibility;
- identity/KYC requirements;
- client payment methods;
- fees;
- withdrawal destination;
- refund/dispute process.

## Rule

Never promise a client a payment method until the account has been verified and tested with the intended business activity.
